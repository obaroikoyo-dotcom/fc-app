FlipCollab

FlipCollab is a creator collaboration marketplace connecting brands with content creators for paid and gifted campaigns. Brands create an account, post campaigns describing the content they need, and review creators who apply. Creators create a profile, browse open campaigns, and apply to the ones that fit their audience and niche. Once a brand accepts a creator, the two message each other directly inside FlipCollab, and for paid campaigns the brand's payment is held securely in escrow via Stripe until the agreed content is delivered.

To provide this service, FlipCollab collects account information, profile details, messages and campaign content, and payment information processed securely through Stripe, used solely to operate accounts, match brands with creators, process payments, and enable messaging - never sold to third parties.
